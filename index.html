Filipe Cavalheiro

Electrical & Computer Engineer · NOVA FCT

I work on cooperative control of drone swarms: how a group of drones can surround a drone that should not be there, evenly and in three dimensions, without colliding.

My tools are model predictive control, control barrier functions, Lyapunov-based geometric control on the sphere and Voronoi-based formations, tested in simulation (PX4, Gazebo, MATLAB) and on real Crazyflie drones. Before that I was making fixed-wing drones, rocket air brakes and small machines that talk to computers over a serial port.

This site is where all of it lives, in detail.
